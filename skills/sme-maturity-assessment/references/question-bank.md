# Original 30-question SME assessment bank

This questionnaire was created for this plugin. It is NOT a reproduction of ENISA's official questionnaire. Domains are People (P), Processes (R), Technology (T).

For every question record: score 0-4/U/NA, evidence/last verified (optional), business relevance, and owner. For NA record justification and exclude from denominator.

## People (P)
P01: Is someone explicitly accountable for cybersecurity decisions and spend?
P02: Are all staff and contractors taught practical phishing/account-security basics at onboarding?
P03: Can staff report suspicious messages or incidents quickly without guessing whom to contact?
P04: Do owners of privileged/financial accounts know how to recognize and challenge suspicious requests?
P05: Are contractor access rights reviewed when their work changes or ends?
P06: Are security responsibilities documented for outsourced IT or SaaS providers?
P07: Is security discussed with company leadership when changes or purchases are proposed?
P08: Do teams practice an incident scenario (e.g., account compromise) at least annually?
P09: Do staff understand rules for handling customer, financial and proprietary data?
P10: Is security training adjusted after incidents, process changes, or identified weaknesses?

## Processes (R)
R01: Is there an up-to-date list of critical business services, systems and data owners?
R02: Is there a recurring process for ranking cyber risks by business impact and likelihood?
R03: Are key legal, privacy, contractual and customer security obligations tracked?
R04: Is there a documented incident response contact list and initial containment process?
R05: Are access requests/approvals and offboarding consistently documented?
R06: Is there a backup and recovery plan with defined recovery needs for critical services?
R07: Are material cloud/SaaS vendors evaluated and reassessed for security dependencies?
R08: Is there a routine to patch, remediate, or accept risks with named owners and deadlines?
R09: Are security-impacting changes reviewed, tested and logged before release?
R10: Are recovery and incident response plans tested, and lessons incorporated into improvements?

## Technology (T)
T01: Is phishing-resistant MFA enabled on privileged accounts and MFA on other critical accounts wherever supported?
T02: Are endpoints and operating systems supported, protected and updated?
T03: Are essential data and settings backed up, with restore tests completed?
T04: Are admin privileges minimized and accounts uniquely attributable to individuals?
T05: Is there a maintained inventory of cloud, SaaS, devices and critical external services?
T06: Are sensitive data encrypted in transit and at rest where applicable?
T07: Are critical cloud/SaaS configurations reviewed against secure defaults?
T08: Do critical systems produce accessible security logs and actionable alerts?
T09: Are publicly exposed systems/dependencies scanned and findings prioritized and remediated?
T10: Are secrets kept in managed stores rather than source code, personal chats or shared documents?

## Quick Baseline
P01-P04, R01-R04, T01-T04 only (12 questions total). Don't compare Quick and Full scores as if equivalent without caveat.
